Not all laptops from the same brand are built equally. Brands make premium series (durable, metallic, great keyboards) alongside cheap budget series (plastic flex, weak hinges).

The Secret Code: CPU Suffix Letters (Intel & AMD)

U Series (e.g., i5-1335U, Ryzen 5 7530U)
Ultra-Low Power / Battery Saver

Produces little heat. Battery lasts 8-12 hours. Excellent for thin-and-light laptops, web dev, and lectures.

P / HS Series (e.g., i5-1340P, Ryzen 7 7840HS)
Balanced Power & Efficiency

The sweet spot for smooth performance without turning your laptop into a hot brick.

H / HX Series (e.g., i7-13700H, Ryzen 7 7745HX)
High Performance Gaming

Maximum power, high fan noise, heavy charger brick. Battery lasts only 2 to 4 hours.

Soldered RAM (Non-Upgradable)

The RAM chip is permanently welded to the motherboard. If you buy an 8GB Soldered laptop, you can NEVER upgrade it. When your CSE coding projects grow in 3rd year, you will be forced to buy a new laptop.

Expandable RAM (SODIMM Slots)

The laptop has removable memory slots. Even if you buy an 8GB model today, you can pop open the back panel and insert a second 8GB stick (~₹1,500) later to instantly turn it into a high performance 16GB laptop.

RED - BAD IN 2026

8GB RAM

Windows 11 takes ~4GB alone. Leaving only 4GB for your apps. Freezes when opening Zoom + Chrome.

GREEN - SWEET SPOT

16GB RAM

The standard requirement for engineering students. Handles web dev, 30 Chrome tabs, and compilation easily.

ORANGE - HEAVY DEV

32GB RAM

Only needed for heavy AI/ML dataset training, Android Studio emulators, and local LLMs.

PURPLE - OVERKILL

64GB+ RAM

Waste of money for college students. Designed for enterprise servers and 3D animation studios.

Webcams: Is 1080p Overhyped?

720p HD Webcam

Looks slightly grainy in dark rooms, but perfectly fine for daily college classes and online vivas. Do NOT pay ₹10k extra just for camera upgrade.

1080p Full HD Webcam

Crisp video quality for job interviews, placements, and video presentation recordings. A nice feature to have.

Physical Privacy Shutter

MUST HAVE feature! A physical slider switch to block the camera lens guarantees total privacy against accidental video turns on.

Screen & Display Panel Guide

RED - AVOID
TN Panel (Twisted Nematic)

Terrible viewing angles. Looking at the screen from slight side angles turns colors completely black or washed out.

GREEN - SWEET SPOT
IPS Panel (Full HD / 300 nits)

Crisp text, wide viewing angles, easy on the eyes during late night coding sessions.

ORANGE - BATTERY HEAVY
OLED Display

Stunning true-black colors for movies, but consumes significantly more battery.
